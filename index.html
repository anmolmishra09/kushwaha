Our Store Story

A Local Destination for Your Style.

Located in the heart of Uttar Mohal, Robertsganj, Uttar Pradesh (231216), Kushwaha Bazar (कुशवाहा बाजार) is dedicated to bringing quality, versatile, and stylish clothing options to our local community.

Whether you are looking for timeless ethnic wear for family celebrations, comfortable casuals for daily life, or celebratory attire, we welcome every visitor with personalized attention and an inviting shopping atmosphere.

We believe in honest customer relationships, attentive service, and an enjoyable hands-on in-store shopping experience where you can browse fabrics, try on your selections, and discover your next favourite look.
